Celebration of Color, Light & Joy

Festivals of India

Diwali

Festival of Lights

Festival of Lights celebrated across India with lamps, sweets and decorations. Celebrated with lights, sweets and traditional rituals.

Holi

Festival of Colors

Famous color festival celebrated with joy, representing happiness and togetherness.

Ganesh Chaturthi

August / September

Celebrated with devotion, cultural events and Ganesh idols. Major festival of Maharashtra with grand celebrations and processions.

Durga Puja

September / October

Major festival of devotion, art and cultural celebrations. One of the biggest festivals of West Bengal.

Pongal

January

Traditional four-day harvest festival of Tamil Nadu celebrated in South India.

Onam

August / September

Kerala's famous traditional festival with boat races, floral decorations and cultural programs.

Baisakhi

April

Important harvest festival celebrated mainly in Punjab with enthusiasm and bhangra dances.

Eid

Islamic Lunar Calendar

Festival of prayers, happiness, feasting, and warm family gatherings.

Dussehra

October

Festival representing the ultimate victory of good over evil, celebrated with Ramlila drama plays.

Lohri

January

Traditional winter and harvest bonfire festival celebrated with folk songs in Punjab.

Janmashtami

August / September

Celebrates the birth of Lord Krishna with Dahi Handi events and devotional singing.

Ugadi

March / April

New Year festival celebrated in Karnataka, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh with traditional dishes.

Vishu

April

Traditional astronomical New Year celebration of Kerala featuring the sacred Vishukkani arrangement.

Mysore Dasara

October

Famous royal 10-day festival of Karnataka featuring grand elephant processions and illuminated palaces.

Rath Yatra

June / July

Famous monumental chariot procession festival of Lord Jagannath in Puri, Odisha.

Bihu

April

Traditional festival of Assam celebrating harvest with lively folk music and graceful dances.

Chhath Puja

October / November

Ancient Sun worship festival celebrated with river offerings and holy fasting in Bihar.

Kali Puja

October / November

Important night festival of West Bengal dedicated to Goddess Kali.

Buddha Purnima

May

Sacred day celebrating the birth, enlightenment, and nirvana of Gautama Buddha.

Navratri

September / October

Famous 9-night festival known for energetic Garba and Dandiya Raas dances in Gujarat.

Gudi Padwa

March / April

Traditional spring New Year festival of Maharashtra celebrated with Gudi flags.

Makar Sankranti

14th January

Solar harvest festival celebrated with colorful kite flying competitions and sesame sweets.
